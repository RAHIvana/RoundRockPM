Text messaging

SMS terms & conditions

Last updated 2026

1. Program description

Round Rock Property Management sends text messages to people who contact us about our property management services, about a rental or commercial space we have listed, or about a property we manage for them. Messages are conversational and transactional: answering your question, confirming a showing, following up on a maintenance request, or sending information you asked for. We do not send marketing blasts.

2. How to join

You consent to receive text messages from us when you text (512) 310-0453 first, when you give us your mobile number and ask us to text you, or when you tick the SMS box on a form on this website. That box is optional and unticked by default.

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of renting a property, applying for a property, or using our management services. You can always reach us by phone or email instead.

3. Message frequency

Message frequency varies and depends on your conversation with us. Most people exchange a handful of messages around a showing, an application, or a repair.

4. Cost

Message and data rates may apply. We do not charge for text messages; your mobile carrier may, according to your plan.

5. How to stop

Reply STOP to any message to opt out at any time. You will receive one confirmation message and then no further texts from that number. You can opt back in later by texting START or by contacting us.

6. How to get help

Reply HELP to any message, or contact us directly:

  • Phone or text: (512) 310-0453
  • Email: support@roundrockpm.com

7. Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery is subject to effective transmission by your mobile carrier and is not guaranteed.

8. Privacy

Your mobile number and the content of your messages are used only to respond to you and to keep a record of your inquiry. No mobile information is sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third parties. See our Privacy Policy for the full detail.

9. Changes

We may update these terms. The current version always lives at this address, with the date it was last changed shown at the top.

These terms are published for transparency and for mobile carrier registration. Have your attorney review them before you rely on them.
