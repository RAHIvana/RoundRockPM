Office & warehouse

Flex space in the 183A corridor

Office-warehouse condo units in Cedar Park, Leander and northwest Austin — climate-controlled warehouse, grade-level roll-up doors, Light Industrial zoning and 24-hour access. Rates are base NNN per square foot per month; NNN is additional.

Text about a space Request details

How the pricing works. The base rate is per square foot per month. NNN (taxes, insurance and common-area maintenance) is charged on top, and electricity and internet are billed separately to the tenant. The “estimated monthly” column is base rent plus the NNN estimate — an estimate only. Final rent depends on the unit, lease term and credit, and is subject to a signed lease.

Zoning is Light Industrial (LI). A specific use — auto repair, fitness, food preparation, assembly, retail walk-in, anything involving hazardous materials — must be approved by both the landlord and the city before a lease is signed. Ask us early and we will get you a straight answer. All information is deemed reliable but not guaranteed; lessee to verify all information.
