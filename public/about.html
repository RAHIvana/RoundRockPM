About us

A local company, run by the people who answer the phone

Round Rock Property Management is owner-operated. There is no regional office, no call center and no hand-off to somebody who has never seen your street.

Our story

Built around one idea

Property ownership should be rewarding, not stressful. We started this company to manage other people's properties the way we manage our own — answering quickly, spending carefully, documenting everything, and telling owners the truth about what a property will actually rent for.

Over the years that has grown into a portfolio of residential and commercial properties across the Greater Austin metro, and a reputation we would rather protect than trade for faster growth.

Our commitment

Transparent, technology-supported management that protects owner assets, minimizes vacancy and creates a living experience residents want to renew — while giving owners their time back.
