Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions

The things owners and residents ask us most. If yours is not here, call, text or email — a real person answers.

Owners

How much does property management cost?

A monthly management fee based on collected rent, plus a leasing fee when a new tenant is placed. The exact figures depend on property type, unit count and condition, so we quote in writing after a free rent analysis rather than posting a number that may not apply to your address. Everything in the quote is what appears on your statement.

What is included in the management fee?

Marketing and showings, tenant screening, lease preparation and renewals, rent collection and disbursement, maintenance coordination, inspections, and monthly financial reporting with year-end tax documents. Optional extras, if any, are listed separately in the agreement so nothing is a surprise later.

How long does it take to lease a property?

It depends on price, condition and season, and anyone who guarantees a number is guessing. What we control is speed of process: photography and listing within days of make-ready, same-day response to inquiries, showings scheduled quickly, and screening turned around before a good applicant drifts elsewhere.

Do I have to approve repairs and tenants?

You set a repair approval threshold; anything above it comes to you with a quote before work starts, except genuine emergencies. On applicants, we screen against written criteria, present the full report, and you decide — with the same standard applied to everyone, as Fair Housing law requires.

Can you manage a property that already has tenants?

Yes. We review the existing lease, introduce ourselves to the residents, migrate rent collection and maintenance onto our systems and document current condition. Existing lease terms remain in force until renewal.

How and when do I get paid?

Owner disbursements go out on a predictable monthly schedule once rent has cleared, with an itemized statement showing income, expenses and the net amount, and contractor invoices attached.

What kinds of property do you manage?

Single-family homes, condos, duplexes and small multi-family, plus commercial space across the Greater Austin metro. If a property is outside our area or outside our competence, we will say so rather than take it on.

Residents

How do I pay rent?

Online through the resident portal by bank transfer or card, with autopay available. Your lease states the due date, any grace period and the late fee that applies.

How do I request a repair?

Use the maintenance form on the Residents page, or the resident portal. For an active leak, no heat in freezing weather, no power, a gas smell, sewage backup or anything unsafe, call us instead — that is what the 24/7 line is for. Fire, gas or immediate danger: call 911 first.

What do I need to qualify for a rental?

Every adult applies and is screened. We look at verifiable income against the rent, credit history, rental history including landlord references, and a background check evaluated individually. Specific thresholds can vary by property and are stated on the listing and application.

How do I get my full security deposit back?

Give proper written notice, leave the home clean and undamaged beyond normal wear, return all keys and remotes, and provide a forwarding address in writing. Texas law then sets the timeline for the refund and itemized statement of deductions.

Can I have a pet?

It depends on the property. Where pets are permitted there may be pet screening and an additional deposit or fee. Assistance animals are handled under federal and Texas law and are not treated as pets.

Can I break my lease early?

Talk to us as soon as you know. Your lease sets out the process and what it costs. Handled early and in writing it is usually manageable; handled by disappearing it is not.

Still have a question?

Ask us directly — we would rather answer it now than have you guess.
