Privacy policy

Last updated 2026

Round Rock Property Management (“we”, “us”) operates roundrockpm.com. This policy explains, in plain English, what we collect through this website, our text messaging and our chat assistant — and what we do not do with it.

What we collect

Only what you give us: the name, email address, phone number, property address and message you enter in a form, type to our chat assistant, or send us by text. If you ask about a specific property we keep a record of which one, so we can follow up properly.

We do not ask for financial account numbers, payment card numbers, or government ID numbers through this website, our chat assistant or by text. Please do not send them to us that way. Rental applications and rent payments are handled through separate secure systems.

Why we collect it

To answer your inquiry, prepare a rent analysis, schedule a showing, process a maintenance request, or respond to a question about a property. We keep inquiry records so we have a history of what was asked and what we answered.

Text messaging (SMS)

If you text us, tick the SMS box on a form, or give us your number and ask us to text you, we will send you text messages about your inquiry or your property. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help. Consent to receive texts is never a condition of renting from us or using our services.

No mobile information is sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties. Your mobile number is used only by us, and by the messaging provider that delivers the message on our behalf, to communicate with you.

Our full SMS Terms & Conditions cover this in detail.

The chat assistant

The assistant on this site is an automated tool, not a person. To produce an answer, the conversation is processed by an AI service provider acting on our behalf. If you give the assistant your contact details, they are sent to our team so we can follow up — that is the point of it. Do not enter payment card numbers, bank details, government ID numbers or other sensitive personal information into the chat; for anything sensitive, call us instead.

The assistant can be wrong. Nothing it says is a binding quote, an approval, or a promise of availability or price. Anything that matters should be confirmed with us directly.

Who sees your information

Our team, and the service providers that operate this website, our email, our text messaging and our property management software on our behalf. They may use it only to provide that service to us. We do not sell your information and we do not share it for anyone else's marketing.

We may disclose information if the law requires it, or where it is necessary to protect someone's safety or our legal rights.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not set advertising cookies and does not track you across other websites. Your browser may store a short chat history locally on your own device so a conversation survives a page refresh; clearing your browser data removes it.

How long we keep it

Inquiry records are kept as long as we need them for the inquiry and for our ordinary business and legal record-keeping. Ask us to delete yours and we will, unless a law or an active lease requires us to retain it.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. You can opt out of texts at any time with STOP, and out of emails by telling us. Email support@roundrockpm.com or call or text (512) 310-0453.

Children

This website is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Fair housing

Round Rock Property Management is an Equal Housing Opportunity provider. We apply the same criteria to every applicant and do not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, national origin, familial status, disability, or any other class protected by federal, state or local law.
