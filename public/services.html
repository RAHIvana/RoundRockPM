Tenant screening

The single decision that determines how the next two years go. We take it seriously and we apply the same standard to every applicant.

  • Credit & financial reviewFull credit report, payment history and debt load, weighed against a documented income standard.
  • Background & eviction searchCriminal background and eviction history across the relevant jurisdictions.
  • Income & employment verificationPay stubs, offer letters or bank records verified directly, not taken at face value.
  • Rental historyWe call previous landlords — the real ones, not the friend listed as a reference.
  • Fair Housing disciplineWritten criteria applied identically to every applicant, every time.

Leasing & compliance

Texas landlord-tenant law is unforgiving about notice, deposits and disclosures. Getting the paperwork right is the cheapest insurance you will ever buy.

  • Texas-compliant lease draftingCurrent lease forms with the disclosures and addenda your property actually requires.
  • Electronic signingLeases, addenda and renewals signed online, stored and retrievable.
  • Move-in / move-out documentationPhotographed condition reports that hold up when a deposit is disputed.
  • Renewals & rent adjustmentsRenewal conversations started early, priced against current market, not last year's number.
  • Notices & deposit handlingStatutory timelines tracked so a technicality never costs you a case.

Maintenance & repairs

Small problems become expensive ones when nobody picks up the phone. Our job is to catch them while they are still small.

  • 24/7 emergency lineA real after-hours path for burst pipes, no heat, no power and security issues.
  • Vetted, insured contractorsLicensed and insured vendors we have used repeatedly, at negotiated rates.
  • Owner approval thresholdsYou set the dollar limit above which we call you first. Emergencies are the only exception.
  • Preventive maintenanceHVAC service, water heater checks and seasonal work scheduled before the failure, not after.
  • Documented work ordersEvery request tracked from report to completion, with invoices attached to your statement.

Rent collection & owner payouts

Predictable money, on a schedule, with the paper trail to back it up.

  • Online tenant paymentsTenants pay by bank transfer or card through a portal, on autopay if they choose.
  • Consistent late-fee enforcementLate notices and fees applied per the lease — consistently, so they hold.
  • Scheduled owner disbursementsFunds released to you on a predictable monthly cadence, with the statement to match.
  • Delinquency handlingEarly contact, documented communication, and a clear escalation path if it does not resolve.

Financial reporting

You should never have to ask where the money went.

  • Itemized monthly statementsIncome, expenses, management fee and net disbursement on one page.
  • Invoices attachedEvery repair charge backed by the contractor's actual invoice.
  • Year-end tax documents1099s and an annual summary your CPA can work from directly.
  • Owner portal accessStatements, documents and work orders available whenever you want to look.

Marketing & leasing

Vacancy is the most expensive line item in the business. Everything here exists to shorten it.

  • Professional photographyProperly lit, wide-angle photos — the first thing that decides whether anyone clicks.
  • Syndicated listingsMLS, Zillow, Apartments.com, Trulia, HotPads and social channels.
  • Market-accurate pricingPriced off current comparable leases, then adjusted quickly if traffic says otherwise.
  • Responsive showingsInquiries answered fast, including by our AI assistant outside business hours.
