What we do

End-to-end management, nothing bolted on

Every service below is part of full management — not an upsell you discover in month three.

Tenant screening

Credit, criminal background, eviction history, income verification and landlord references — applied consistently and within Fair Housing rules.

Leasing & compliance

Texas-compliant lease drafting, e-signatures, renewals, rent adjustments and documented move-in / move-out condition reports.

Maintenance & repairs

A 24/7 emergency line, a vetted network of licensed and insured contractors, and preventive work that heads off expensive failures.

Rent collection

Online payments, consistent late-fee enforcement, and owner disbursements on a predictable monthly schedule.

Financial reporting

Itemized monthly statements, maintenance invoices attached, and year-end 1099 documents your CPA will not complain about.

Marketing & leasing

Professional photos and syndicated listings on the MLS, Zillow, Apartments.com and social — built to cut days on market.
